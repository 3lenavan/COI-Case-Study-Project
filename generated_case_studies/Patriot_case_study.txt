**Client Overview**

Patriot Family Insurance is the client for the Nashua Office Renovation project.

**Project Challenge**

The client's actual workspace needs include a need for a private office desk, mobile file cabinet, conference room table, and conference chairs. The client's occupancy or headcount goal is 90-100 agents. The client is concerned about the layout of the office, including the placement of cubicles, chairs, and glass partitions. The client also wants to ensure that the office is acoustically comfortable and has adequate technology infrastructure.

**COI Solution**

Commercial Office Interiors (COI) proposed a solution that includes:

* Private office desks
* Mobile file cabinets
* Conference room tables
* Conference chairs
* Acoustic partitions
* Technology infrastructure, including dual monitors and task chairs

**Products and Design Decisions**

COI recommended the following products and design decisions:

* Private office desks: 4 units, $850 each
* Mobile file cabinets: 4 units, $425 each
* Conference room tables: 1 unit, $2,500
* Conference chairs: 8 units, $475 each
* Acoustic partitions: to be determined
* Technology infrastructure: dual monitors and task chairs

**Project Results**

The project is currently in the design phase. The client has not yet confirmed the final design or scope of work.
